Duck.ai now lets users share individual responses or full conversations through links that recipients can read, continue, or save without an account. Chats are encrypted before upload, with the decryption key stored in the link’s URL fragment, and DuckDuckGo says it cannot read the shared content. Links are unlisted and expire after 48 hours by default, with options for 1, 2, 7, or 30 days, or earlier deletion through Shared Chats settings.
