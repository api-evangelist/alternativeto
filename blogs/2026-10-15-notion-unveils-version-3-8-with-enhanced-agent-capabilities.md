Notion has launched version 3.8, introducing a suite of features designed to strengthen collaboration between human teams and AI agents . The update centers on “context for your agents” , with major improvements to synced databases now supporting GitHub , Jira , and Salesforce with live health status monitoring. Perhaps the most anticipated addition is column-level permissions for databases, allowing owners to control which specific properties individuals or agents can view or edit, a feature reportedly requested by users for years.
