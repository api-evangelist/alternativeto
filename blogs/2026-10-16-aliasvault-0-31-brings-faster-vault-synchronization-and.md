AliasVault has released version 0.31 , capping roughly four months of development with what the project describes as its “long-awaited major vault data model update” . According to the official announcement , the release introduces a rebuilt vault architecture and a new V2 API aimed at making synchronization substantially faster and more reliable , alongside what the project characterizes as additional improvements to vault security. The update also lays groundwork for features still to come.
