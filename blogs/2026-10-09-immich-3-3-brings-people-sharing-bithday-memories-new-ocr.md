Photo management platform Immich has released version 3.3 , introducing several workflow-focused improvements aimed at making personal photo libraries more collaborative and intelligent. The headline addition is person-sharing capabilities within cluster groups , allowing users to share face recognition data with others. Shared persons now appear in both users' contact lists across mobile and web interfaces, with editable name and birthdate fields that sync across the group by default, a notable step toward multi-user photo organization.
