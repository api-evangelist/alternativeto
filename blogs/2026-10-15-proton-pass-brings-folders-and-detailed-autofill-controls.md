Proton Pass now supports folders and nested subfolders for organizing vault items, including drag-and-drop, filtering, and search within folders. Autofill URL matching can be customized with domain, host, wildcard, regular-expression, and never-fill rules, alongside controls for which fields are filled. The update also adds local compromised-password detection, alerting users when a password fingerprint matches known breach data.
