COSMIC Epoch 1.10.0 adds settings for mapping drawing tablets to specific displays. The release also fixes compositor issues involving focus, tiling resize behavior, context menus, screen capture, touchscreen input, and hybrid NVIDIA graphics. COSMIC Store gains a screenshot gallery with local caching, while COSMIC Launcher moves to WGPU rendering.
