Ghost has added flow-level analytics for welcome automations, showing runs over time and how many members are in progress, completed the flow, or stopped early. The Automations tab also shows when each flow last triggered, its total runs, and its current in-progress members. Users can filter results by date range or search for a member to view their completed steps, sent emails, and upcoming actions on the automation canvas.
