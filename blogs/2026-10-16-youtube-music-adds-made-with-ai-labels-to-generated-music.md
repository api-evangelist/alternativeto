YouTube Music is introducing a new “Made with AI” label for music tracks that were substantially altered or fully generated using generative AI. The label focuses on songs where AI played a significant role in creating elements such as compositions or vocals, rather than tracks that simply used AI tools during production. The system follows the same general approach as YouTube’s existing AI disclosure labels for videos.
