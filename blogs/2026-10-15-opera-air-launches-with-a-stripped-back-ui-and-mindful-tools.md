Opera has launched a reimagined Opera Air browser with no Speed Dials, widgets, predefined shortcuts, or promotional notifications enabled by default. The browser includes Soundscapes in Focus, Work, Relax, and Ambience categories, along with optional binaural beats, timers, repeat controls, and five new tracks including Hyperfocus. Its updated Breaks feature offers guided breathing and neck exercises, customizable voice and audio settings, and optional reminders from 45 to 180 minutes.
