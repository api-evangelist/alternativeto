Fastmail has rolled out what it describes as “a big update” to its calendar, headlined by a new world clock feature aimed at users who coordinate across time zones. In a post on the company's blog, Chief Product Officer Neil Jenkins said the release lets customers keep other people's local time visible while planning their day. Users can add locations in the settings; those clocks then appear in the desktop calendar sidebar beneath the mini calendar, and clicking one displays the entire calendar in that time zone.
