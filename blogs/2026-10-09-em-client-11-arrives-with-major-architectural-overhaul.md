Email client eM Client has officially released version 11 , marking one of the most significant updates in the software's history. The new version introduces native support for both ARM and 64-bit processors , delivering improved performance for large mailboxes and more battery-efficient operation on ARM-based devices. Under the hood, eM Client 11 now runs on a new Chromium render core, making the application faster, more responsive, and better equipped to handle complex message rendering.
