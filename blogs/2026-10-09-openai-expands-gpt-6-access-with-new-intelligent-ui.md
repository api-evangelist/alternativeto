OpenAI has announced the widespread availability of GPT-6 , marking a significant expansion of its most advanced AI model beyond premium subscribers. While initial GPT-6 access was restricted to paid customers last month, the company now brings the technology to ChatGPT 's entire user base, including Free and Go tier users. The release introduces GPT-6 Luna for free-tier users and maintains GPT-6 Sol for Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise accounts, both calibrated for everyday conversation.
