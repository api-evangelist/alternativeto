Sync-in has released version 2.6 of its collaborative platform, headlined by the integration of draw.io , the popular open-source diagram editor. According to the company's official announcement , this addition represents a much-requested feature that completes the platform's suite of editing tools. The move aligns with Sync-in's stated goal of building a comprehensive, open collaborative environment free from dependency on proprietary software solutions.
