Ensu now offers downloadable knowledge packs that use local RAG search to retrieve passages from selected Wikipedia datasets and Wikibooks before answering factual questions. Pack-based answers include source attribution, and Ensu says the feature improved factual-question results in tests of 500 questions, particularly with smaller models. The app also lets users add a folder of Markdown notes for local indexing and retrieval, with edits typically detected within 5 to 10 minutes while the app is open.
